# START HERE — what this repository is

This repository collects **receipted primary sources** and **clearly-labeled third-party
derivations** relevant to one question in the study of consciousness:

> **Where does a "bit" of information come from, if spacetime is an interface?**

It is a fan-run research collection honoring Donald Hoffman's published program. It is
**not affiliated with or endorsed by** Prof. Hoffman. Everything speculative here is
marked speculative.

---

## The published results this builds on

All of these are peer-reviewed, and each is summarized with its citation in
`docs/receipts-and-sources.md` (the raw abstracts we quote are fetched, not paraphrased):

1. **Fitness Beats Truth (FBT) Theorem** — Prakash, Stephens, Hoffman & Singh (2021),
   *[Acta Biotheoretica](https://link.springer.com/journal/404)*. Natural selection drives truthful perceptions extinct against
   perceptions tuned only to fitness. Spacetime, as perceived, is a species-specific
   interface — a headset — not a window onto objective reality.
2. **Objects of Consciousness** — Hoffman & Prakash (2014). A formal model of "conscious
   agents" in which physical objects *emerge from* the dynamics of interacting agents —
   including a correspondence with the quantum free particle.
3. **Fusions of Consciousness** — Hoffman, Prakash & Prentner (2023). Conscious agents
   are placed *beyond spacetime*; a particle in spacetime is a projection of the agents'
   Markovian dynamics; scattering amplitudes are connected to agent interactions.
4. **Interfacing Consciousness** — Prentner & Hoffman (2024). "We live inside a
   simulation instantiated, not digitally, but in consciousness."

## The gap this repository works on

The it-from-bit tradition says every physical thing derives from yes/no questions posed
and registered. The interface results say the register is a headset. But then: **where
does the BIT itself come from — before any headset renders it?**

Our proposed completion (speculative, tagged [EXTENSION] wherever it appears):

> **I = E + O.** Information = Energy + Operation/Observation.

- **E (energy)** alone is undifferentiated — no bits, just everything equally present.
- **O (operation/observation)** is the act of taking a difference *as* a difference —
  issuing a metric, selecting one state against alternatives.
- **I (information)** is the sum: a bit is *manufactured, in the act*, by an observer
  running an operation on energy. There is no observation from nowhere.

Two consequences we develop in `docs/`:

- **The rendering is the descent.** An observer cannot render a world without being
  rendered inside it (the headset always renders the head wearing it). Embodiment is
  therefore not a special case of consciousness but a consequence of wanting information.
- **The recursive trace.** Attempts to place mind inside the brain find the brain is an
  icon inside the mind; the trace never exits consciousness. We reconstruct this from the
  published interface results and tag the reconstruction as ours.
