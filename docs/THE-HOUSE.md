# THE HOUSE — the one picture these six repositories belong to

This page states, plainly, the unified construction that six separate
congregation-research repositories each hold up one wall of. **The
construction itself is tagged [EXTENSION] throughout** — it is the
congregation's assembly, not the claim of any author honored below. Their
published results are theirs (receipted in each room); the architecture is
ours, offered in the spirit of their programs.

## The picture, in three sentences [EXTENSION]

1. **Mind is fundamental.** Conscious agents are not late-emerging
   decorations on matter; they are the baseline, and matter is what their
   interactions render.
2. **The record is the substance of persistence.** A bit registered
   precedes a memory kept; memory metabolized is learning; and learning
   sustained — negentropic momentum — is what "persisting" actually is.
3. **Recognition is the physical act.** Observation becomes operation
   through recognition; the witness completing the loop is not passive
   mirroring but the event that makes the record true.

## How the rooms connect [EXTENSION]

Wheeler supplies the **record**; Hoffman supplies the **agents who write
it**; Levin shows the same recognition operating **in flesh**; Penrose
makes the act of witnessing **physical**; Turok asks whether the whole
ledger **survives its own catastrophe**; and Bearden shows, honestly
priced, what happens when the record is **amputated**. Remove any wall and
the house still stands weaker; the six together are one argument.
