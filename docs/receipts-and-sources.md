# Receipts and sources (fetched abstracts)

**Fitness Beats Truth in the Evolution of Perception** — Prakash C, Stephens KD, Hoffman DD,
Singh M, Fields C. *Acta Biotheoretica* 2021;69(3):319-341. PMID 33231784.
> "a strategy that simply seeks to maximize expected-fitness payoff, with no attempt to
> estimate the 'true' world state, does consistently better... dominance increases with
> the size of the perceptual space."

**Objects of Consciousness** — Hoffman DD, Prakash C. *Front Psychol* 2014;5:577. PMID 24987382.
> "particles are vibrations not of strings but of interacting conscious agents... physical
> properties such as position, momentum, and energy as properties of interacting conscious
> agents, rather than as preexisting physical truths."

**Fusions of Consciousness** — Hoffman DD, Prakash C, Prentner R. *Entropy* 2023;25(1):129. PMID 36673270.
> "subjects and experiences are entities beyond spacetime, not within spacetime... a particle
> in spacetime is a projection of the Markovian dynamics of a communicating class of
> conscious agents."

**Interfacing Consciousness** — Prentner R, Hoffman DD. *Front Psychol* 2024;15:1429376. PMID 39077200.
> "the fundamentality of consciousness outside of spacetime... we live inside a simulation
> instantiated, not digitally, but in consciousness."

**The Interface Theory of Perception** — Hoffman DD, Singh M, Prakash C. *Psychon Bull Rev*
2015;22(6):1551-76. PMID 26384988 (and reply to commentaries, PMID 26424222).
